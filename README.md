# Subnetsomm-lab

“The digital cellar of me, subnetsomm, exploring cybersecurity, networking, and everything in between. A work-in-progress, just like a good fermentation.”

Welcome to the digital cellar. I’m Franco Pisano, and this is a curated record of my cybersecurity, networking, systems and programming coursework at Northern Virginia Community College.

The entries follow the original work: what the lab was for, the tools and workflow, what the saved submission records, and the learning behind the result. Guided exercises, original programs and design projects are labeled so their scope is clear.

## Reading the evidence

This first public edition contains narrative write-ups and selected original source code. The 32 platform lab exports in my archive record 100% progress; that indicator does not independently validate every result. Unreviewed screenshots, raw forensic images/captures, personal details, exam materials and supplied course worksheets are kept out of these public entries. The capstone assessment includes separately reviewed screenshots.

Learning objectives identify the skills practiced; each write-up explains the observations or limitations supported by its saved submission. Suggested improvements are separated from historical work. The Python source preserves its coursework form and is not presented as production software.

See [publication notes](PUBLICATION-NOTES.md) for provenance and editorial scope, and my existing [learning roadmap](learning-roadmap) for broader interests.
