# Threat likelihood and impact assessment

**Author:** Franco Pisano · **Context:** ITN 262 · Summer 2026 · **Type:** Guided lab

## Purpose

Practice translating threat scenarios into structured risk records.

## Learning objectives

- Distinguish threat, asset, likelihood and impact.
- State assumptions behind qualitative risk judgments.
- Record a rationale that another reviewer can challenge.

## Workflow

1. Identify assets and an adversarial threat in SimpleRisk.
2. Assess likelihood and impact with supporting notes.
3. Repeat the workflow for a non-adversarial threat.
4. Develop and assess an internal-adversary scenario.

## Results and learning

The report includes submitted risk records and a written internal-threat scenario. The value is the documented reasoning, not the appearance of numerical precision: likelihood and impact assessments depend on stated access, intent, conditions and uncertainty.
