# Evidence integrity and chain of custody

**Author:** Franco Pisano · **Context:** ITN 276 · Spring 2026 · **Type:** Guided lab

## Purpose

Practice preserving, examining and documenting digital evidence in a forensic workflow.

## Learning objectives

- Explain the roles of evidence integrity and provenance.
- Connect recovered artifacts with their original locations.
- Document examination steps so another analyst can review them.

## Workflow

1. Review the supplied warrant and complete chain-of-custody procedures.
2. Examine the provided evidence and document recovered artifacts.
3. Verify evidence-image hashes at the command line.
4. Identify original names and paths for additional recovered files.

## Results and learning

The submission records hash-verification and artifact-location tasks. The important distinction is between preserving evidence and interpreting it: a matching hash supports integrity of the compared bytes; it does not, by itself, establish a complete chain of custody or decide legal admissibility.
