**Author:** Franco Pisano · **Context:** ITN 261 · Summer 2026 · **Type:** Guided lab

## Purpose

Study how egress policy affects a controlled payload-delivery exercise.

## Learning objectives

- Relate outbound policy to simulated compromise.
- Document delivery and session establishment separately.
- Identify defensive opportunities across the workflow.

## Workflow

1. Inspect the lab’s egress-filtering behavior.
2. Complete the supplied payload creation and delivery workflow.
3. Record the resulting lab session and identity output.
4. Compare the challenge payload with the initial exercise.
